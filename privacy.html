Who we are

Finboard is a personal budgeting app for Android and iOS (app ID com.aral.finboard), made by Aral Benli, an independent developer. In this policy "we" means the developer. Contact: support@aralbenli.com.

1. Data that stays on your device

  • Everything you enter: income, expenses, fixed costs, cards, installments, funds, assets, ledgers, categories, notes, reports and settings.
  • Receipt and document photos you attach. They are shrunk on the device and stored inside the same database.
  • The database is encrypted: on Android with SQLCipher, on iOS with the system's Data Protection. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app.
  • Finboard has no server and no user account and does not connect to your bank. We cannot see this data, and deleting the app deletes it.

2. Backups

When you create a backup, the app writes one file encrypted with a password you choose (AES-256, the key is derived from your password). You decide where the file goes, for example iCloud Drive, Google Drive or your device. We never receive it and cannot open it. If you forget the password, the backup cannot be restored.

3. App lock

The optional PIN and biometric lock work entirely on the device. Fingerprint and face data are handled by your phone's operating system; the app never sees them.

4. Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

  • Firebase Analytics tells us which screens and features are used, for example that a report was opened, a backup was saved or the simple view was chosen. It also sends the app language, the country and home currency you picked in Settings, your plan (free or Premium) and how many records of a type you have, only as ranges such as "4–6".
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report when the app crashes: the error, the app version, device model and operating system.
  • Both use a random app-instance ID created by Firebase. They never receive amounts (not even ranges), titles, notes, category names or your lock code.
  • We do not use the advertising ID, and ad personalization is turned off.
  • Google stores this data on our behalf. Analytics data is kept for at most 14 months and crash reports for 90 days.

5. Purchases (RevenueCat, Google Play, App Store)

Premium is sold through Google Play or the App Store, which process the payment. We never see your card details. RevenueCat manages the subscription status for us. It receives your purchase history (product, date, price, country of the store account) and a random app user ID that is not linked to your name or email.

6. Feedback you send

If you use Settings → Feedback, the app sends your message to our support server (feedback.aralbenli.com, running on Cloudflare), which emails it to us. It contains:

  • your message, and your email address only if you type one so we can reply;
  • app version, platform, operating system, device model, app language, the country chosen in Settings, whether you have Premium and your RevenueCat app user ID, so we can check a purchase problem;
  • the server also estimates your country from your IP address.

Feedback never includes your financial data. We keep these emails only as long as needed to answer you, and delete them on request.

7. Exchange rates and prices

To value foreign-currency records and assets, the app downloads public rates from the European Central Bank via Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev) and from Truncgil Finans (finans.truncgil.com). The app sends no personal data with these requests. As with any internet request, these services can see your IP address.

8. Permissions

  • Notifications: reminders you set up, scheduled on your device.
  • Face ID / biometrics: only for the optional app lock.
  • Camera and photos: only when you attach a receipt. On Android and iOS the system photo picker is used, so the app sees only the photos you pick.
  • Finboard does not ask for location, contacts, SMS or access to other apps' notifications.

9. No ads, no selling, no tracking

Finboard shows no ads. We do not sell or rent your data and do not use it to track you across other apps or websites.

To find your records, include your RevenueCat ID (shown in the feedback details) or the email you wrote from. Deleting the app removes all data stored on the device. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

1. Daten, die auf Ihrem Gerät bleiben

  • Alles, was Sie eingeben: Einnahmen, Ausgaben, Fixkosten, Karten, Raten, Fonds, Vermögenswerte, Bücher, Kategorien, Notizen, Berichte und Einstellungen.
  • Beleg- und Dokumentfotos, die Sie anhängen. Sie werden auf dem Gerät verkleinert und in derselben Datenbank gespeichert.
  • Die Datenbank ist verschlüsselt: unter Android mit SQLCipher, unter iOS mit dem Datenschutz (Data Protection) des Systems. Die automatische Cloud-Sicherung von Android ist für diese App deaktiviert.
  • Finboard hat keinen Server und kein Benutzerkonto und verbindet sich nicht mit Ihrer Bank. Wir können diese Daten nicht sehen; wenn Sie die App löschen, werden sie gelöscht.

2. Sicherungen

Wenn Sie eine Sicherung erstellen, schreibt die App eine Datei, die mit einem von Ihnen gewählten Passwort verschlüsselt ist (AES-256, der Schlüssel wird aus Ihrem Passwort abgeleitet). Sie entscheiden, wo die Datei gespeichert wird, zum Beispiel in iCloud Drive, Google Drive oder auf Ihrem Gerät. Wir erhalten sie nie und können sie nicht öffnen. Wenn Sie das Passwort vergessen, kann die Sicherung nicht wiederhergestellt werden.

Die optionale PIN- und biometrische Sperre arbeitet vollständig auf dem Gerät. Fingerabdruck- und Gesichtsdaten verarbeitet das Betriebssystem Ihres Telefons; die App sieht sie nie.

Außerdem werden die App-Sprache, das in den Einstellungen gewählte Land und die Hauptwährung, Ihr Tarif (kostenlos oder Premium) und die Anzahl Ihrer Einträge eines Typs gesendet, nur als Bereiche wie „4–6“.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sendet bei einem Absturz einen Absturzbericht: den Fehler, die App-Version, das Gerätemodell und das Betriebssystem.
  • Beide verwenden eine zufällige App-Instanz-ID von Firebase. Sie erhalten nie Beträge (auch keine Bereiche), Titel, Notizen, Kategorienamen oder Ihren Sperrcode.
